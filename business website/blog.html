Choosing Popcorn for Your Next Event

Published on: 26 November 2026

Planning Ahead

Events are opportunities to create memorable experiences for guests. Snacks can form part of that experience.

Before making an event order, consider the following:

  • The number of expected guests.
  • Your preferred popcorn flavours.
  • The estimated quantity required.
  • Your event date and location.

Contact KJB Popcorn early to discuss your event requirements and availability.
